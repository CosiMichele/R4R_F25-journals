## Research Application

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Due Monday 29th, 11pm**

I think there is a lot of opportunity of LLMs to cut down on time spent transcribing data sheets, which would also, ideally, significantly reduce human error. Research Rabbit seems incredibly helpful because it offers the map, providing a visualization of where terminology has been used before. That is particularly interesting, because in my lab we are studying interactions. That means we might end up proving something in our research, that is common knowledge in a more specific field. For example, we might examine an ant-seed interaction and derive what we believe to be new information about ant preferences. But if we had checked with entomologists first, we would have learned that preference was common knowledge. Having thsi map, helpes us get various perspectives, and helps us identify where the research gaps are.
