## Research Application

This week’s Open Science session taught me that transparency, sharing, and reproducibility are essential for trustworthy research. I learned that making data and code openly available increases visibility, collaboration, and validation opportunities for other scientists. In biomedical engineering, I can apply these concepts by using FAIR data practices and sharing methods or simulation scripts that don’t involve confidential information. The principle “as open as possible, as closed as necessary” reminded me that ethical responsibility should always guide how I share information. Moving forward, I plan to incorporate Open Science behaviors, like documentation, open data management, and code transparency, to contribute to a more reproducible and collaborative research environment.
